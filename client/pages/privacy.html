Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, post a rental listing, submit property inquiries, or communicate with listing owners. This includes your name, email address, and phone number.

2. Protection of User Information

Inquiry messages and viewing requests are managed strictly through the portal. To safeguard user privacy, contact details such as phone numbers and emails are not publicly displayed on public search and listing pages.

3. Cookies and Authentication

We utilize secure, HttpOnly session cookies to manage authenticated sessions securely against cross-site scripting vulnerabilities.

4. Platform Operations Notice

The platform provides listing discovery and inquiry coordination only. The portal does not process lease agreements, background verifications, escrow deposits, or rental payment transactions.
